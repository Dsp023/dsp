Our Story

Food is best
when it's honest

Ember began with a single hearth and a simple belief: that great food doesn't need theatre — just care, quality ingredients, and time.

Our kitchen works with farmers and fishers within 200 km. The menu changes when ingredients peak. Nothing is frozen. Everything is made here.
