Example: Riya's Skin Clinic — Hyderabad

Riya runs a skin clinic with 3 staff. She gets 20–30 enquiries a week through Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, and a Google form. Most of them fall through because no one has time to follow up.

This is what her process looks like right now — and what it looks like after we set up one automation workflow.

❌ Before Automation
✅ After Automation
Step 01
📱
Lead comes in — nobody notices
A potential patient fills a Google Form or sends a WhatsApp. It gets buried among 40 other messages. Riya checks it the next morning — sometimes later.
Avg. response: 6–18 hours
↕
Step 01
⚡
Lead comes in — instant response
The moment the form is submitted, an automated WhatsApp message goes out: "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! We'll confirm your slot within the hour." Lead feels seen.
Response time: under 60 seconds
Step 02
📓
Details written in a notebook
Name, phone, treatment interest — written by hand or copy-pasted into a WhatsApp chat. No structure. Impossible to search or track.
